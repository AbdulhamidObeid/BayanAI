# Islamic Terminology Guide — Canonical Translation Rules
# For use in Gemini system prompts
# Source: Al-Jamharah Islamic Content Dictionary (Bathel Foundation, LOCKED)
# =========================================================================
# Every term below has:
#   - The Arabic term
#   - The FORBIDDEN translations (never use these)
#   - The REQUIRED canonical gloss (always use this form or a close equivalent)

## Core Theological Terms

**التوحيد (Tawheed)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Monotheism" alone, "Unity of God" alone
- REQUIRED: "Tawheed (the exclusive Oneness of Allah — no partner, no equal, no intermediary)"

**الجهاد (Jihad)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Holy War", "warfare", "Islamic military campaign"
- REQUIRED: "Jihad (striving in the path of Allah — includes inner spiritual struggle, scholarly effort, and righteous action)"

**الزكاة (Zakah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Tax", "Islamic tax", "charity donation", "tithe"
- REQUIRED: "Zakah (the obligatory annual purification of wealth — one of the Five Pillars of Islam)"

**التقوى (Taqwa)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Fear of God" alone, "piety" alone, "God-fearing"
- REQUIRED: "Taqwa (mindful God-consciousness — being aware of Allah in all actions and abstaining from what displeases Him)"

**البركة (Barakah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Good luck", "blessing" alone, "karma", "divine luck"
- REQUIRED: "Barakah (divine grace and increase — a blessed quality Allah places in people, time, food, or places)"

**الدعوة (Dawah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Proselytism", "conversion effort", "Islamic propaganda", "missionary work"
- REQUIRED: "Dawah (the invitation to understand Islam — sharing knowledge with wisdom and good counsel)"

**الشريعة (Sharia)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic law" alone without context, "penal code", "harsh law"
- REQUIRED: "Sharia (the comprehensive divine guidance covering worship, ethics, family, commerce, and governance in Islam)"

**الفقه (Fiqh)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic law" (confusing with Sharia), "rules"
- REQUIRED: "Fiqh (Islamic jurisprudence — the scholarly interpretation and application of Islamic principles to real-life situations)"

**الإجماع (Ijma)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Consensus" alone without context
- REQUIRED: "Ijma (scholarly consensus — the unanimous agreement of qualified Islamic scholars)"

**الاجتهاد (Ijtihad)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic opinion", "guess", "interpretation"
- REQUIRED: "Ijtihad (scholarly independent reasoning — deriving rulings from sources where there is no explicit text)"

**الفتوى (Fatwa)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Death sentence", "Islamic decree against someone", "religious attack order"
- REQUIRED: "Fatwa (a formal legal opinion issued by a qualified Islamic scholar on a specific question)"

**الإيمان (Iman)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Faith" alone (incomplete), "belief" alone
- REQUIRED: "Iman (faith in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Day of Judgment, and divine decree)"

**الإسلام (Islam)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Submission" alone without explanation, "Arab religion"
- REQUIRED: "Islam (complete submission to the will of Allah — a comprehensive way of life revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ)"

**السنة (Sunnah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic tradition", "habits of Muhammad"
- REQUIRED: "Sunnah (the recorded sayings, actions, and approvals of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — the second source of Islamic law after the Quran)"

**الحديث (Hadith)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic saying", "Muhammad's words"  
- REQUIRED: "Hadith (a narration reporting the sayings or actions of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, with an authenticated chain of narrators)"

**الصحابة (Sahabah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Muhammad's followers", "early Muslims" only
- REQUIRED: "Sahabah (the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ — those who met him while believing and died as Muslims)"

**الخلافة (Khilafah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "ISIS", "Islamic empire", "caliphate" alone with negative connotation
- REQUIRED: "Khilafah (Islamic governance system — leadership of the Muslim community following the Prophetic model)"

**الصلاة (Salah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Prayer" alone (acceptable but incomplete), "Muslim prayer ritual"
- REQUIRED: "Salah (the five daily prayers — a direct connection between the worshipper and Allah, one of the Five Pillars)"

**الصوم / الصيام (Sawm)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic diet", "Muslim starving"
- REQUIRED: "Sawm (fasting — abstaining from food, drink, and marital relations from dawn to sunset during Ramadan)"

**الحج (Hajj)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic pilgrimage" alone, "Mecca trip"
- REQUIRED: "Hajj (the pilgrimage to Makkah — one of the Five Pillars, obligatory once in a lifetime for those able)"

**الشهادة (Shahada)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic password", "Muslim motto"
- REQUIRED: "Shahada (the declaration of faith: 'There is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and Muhammad is His messenger')"

**الآخرة (Akhirah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Afterlife" alone, "Islamic heaven"
- REQUIRED: "Akhirah (the Hereafter — the eternal life after death, encompassing the Day of Judgment, Paradise, and Hellfire)"

**الجنة (Jannah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Heaven", "paradise" alone without Islamic context
- REQUIRED: "Jannah (Paradise — the eternal abode of reward for the believers in the Hereafter)"

**النار / جهنم (Jahannam)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic hell", "fire punishment" without context
- REQUIRED: "Jahannam (Hellfire — the place of punishment in the Hereafter for those who rejected Allah)"

**القدر (Qadar)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Fate", "Islamic determinism", "God's will" alone
- REQUIRED: "Qadar (divine decree — Allah's knowledge and will over all that exists and occurs, while humans retain moral responsibility)"

**النية (Niyyah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic intention", "religious purpose"
- REQUIRED: "Niyyah (intention — the inward resolve of the heart before an act of worship, which determines its validity and reward)"

**الوضوء (Wudu)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Islamic washing", "Muslim ritual wash"
- REQUIRED: "Wudu (ritual purification — the specific ablution performed before prayer using clean water)"

**المسجد (Masjid)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Mosque" without clarification (acceptable but Masjid is more accurate)
- REQUIRED: "Masjid (a place of prostration — the Muslim house of worship)" or just "Masjid"

**الكعبة (Kaaba)**
- FORBIDDEN: "House of Allah" implying Allah lives there, "idol of Muslims"
- REQUIRED: "the Kaaba (the cubic structure in Makkah — the qibla direction Muslims face in prayer, built by Ibrahim ﷺ)"

**القبلة (Qiblah)**
- FORBIDDEN: "Direction of Mecca", "Muslim prayer direction"
- REQUIRED: "Qiblah (the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah — the unified direction Muslims face during Salah worldwide)"

---

## Instructions for Use
When generating any Islamic content in any language:
1. When you must translate one of the above terms, always use the REQUIRED form.
2. You may use the English translation AFTER introducing the Arabic term and its canonical gloss.
3. Never use a FORBIDDEN translation in any context.
4. In Arabic responses, keep the Arabic terms as-is — no translation needed.
5. In non-Arabic responses, introduce the Arabic term first, then give its canonical gloss in parentheses.
