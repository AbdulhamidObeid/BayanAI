## Simple recording steps

1. Open the video and the script side by side.
2. Use a quiet room and record your voice on your phone or computer. Start speaking at the first frame.
3. Leave a small pause between sentences. Match the timestamps; do not read fast.
4. Add your voice to the existing video in your editor. Keep the original website captures and the saved-answer labels.
5. Export 1920×1080 MP4. Check that the total is 1:56 and the voice is clear.

If recording a new live demo instead: open Bayan, choose Arabic and General, enter the beginner question, and click Send. Record the actual wait. Use the History page to open the already reviewed advanced, regional and multilingual comparisons. Explain that they are saved comparisons. You may cut long waiting time, but label the cut; do not make it look like fresh reasoning finished instantly.

## English meaning of the story

Bayan explains trusted Islamic sources in a way the reader can understand. It uses the question to judge how much detail is needed. It changes the explanation's starting point for different audiences. It can answer an Arabic question in English and show an Arabic companion with source passages. Its checks take time because it retrieves evidence and reviews the explanation. Our recorded local run passed all 12 example test categories and 41 checks.
